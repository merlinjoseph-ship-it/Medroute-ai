System Architecture Workflow

How our automated decision-support pipeline works from incident to hospital arrival.

1. Triage & Vitals Analysis

Analyzes vital signs like Heart Rate, Blood Pressure, SpO2, and Age using a trained Random Forest classifier to instantly determine emergency severity (Low, Medium, High).

2. Smart Hospital Filtering

Automatically filters local hospital databases based on clinical needs—matching high-severity cases strictly to facilities with available ICU beds and Trauma units.

3. Route & Map Mapping

Computes geographic distance via Haversine logic and renders an interactive map showing the incident point, target hospital, and connecting transit path.
